meet morgane delvaux

Belgian by origin, Dubai by choice. Morgane Delvaux built Maison Delvaux with a singular conviction: that true luxury is not about price tags — it is about access, timing, and discretion.

With years of experience at the intersection of haute couture, hospitality, and exclusive networks, Morgane personally oversees every client relationship. No request is too complex, and no detail too small.

Her network spans from the Burj Al Arab to the private islands of the Maldives, from the ateliers of Milan to the beach clubs of Monaco.
